Digital App Settings & Guidelines

STRICT DIGITAL RULES

No Hints & Move Shows

In-app movement highlights, legal square suggestions, analysis hints, and takebacks are completely disabled.

Equal App Timer Control

Every game is configured with an identical fixed time clock per player, managed strictly by the chess platform software.

Platform Based Gameplay

Players are present physically at the venue, but play digitally on Android devices instead of using physical boards.

Optimized Fair Schedule (Every player plays exactly 3 group matches + Semi 1, Semi 2, and Final)

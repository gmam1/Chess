Tournament Specifications

RULES & FORMAT

Match Time Control

Total match time is 20 minutes (10 minutes per player) managed strictly using a digital stopwatch.

  • 10 Minutes per Player
  • Digital Stopwatch Timer

Illegal Turn Rule

Making an illegal turn or a wrong move that can never be turned results in an immediate elimination ("godi will out").

  • Zero Tolerance for Irreversible Errors
  • Immediate Out / Elimination

Daily Timing

2 matches are held per matchday. First match starts promptly at 12:00 PM and second match at 12:30 PM.

  • Match 1: 12:00 PM
  • Match 2: 12:30 PM
